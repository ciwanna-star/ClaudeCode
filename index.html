Important Product Safety Recall Notice

Recall Initiated: September 3, 2026

Nanaimo HVAC is notifying customers of a manufacturer safety recall affecting specific Daikin/Amana air conditioner and heat pump units. If you own one of the affected models listed below, please stop use of the unit if you notice any unusual operation and contact us or the manufacturer immediately to schedule a free inspection and repair.

Model Number Product Type
PBC092J00AA Air Conditioner
PBC122J00AA Air Conditioner
PBC123J00AA Air Conditioner
PBH092J12AA Heat Pump Additional Model

What to do if your unit is affected

  1. Locate the model number on the manufacturer's rating plate, typically on the side or back of the unit.
  2. If your model matches the list above, discontinue use only if you observe unusual noise, odor, smoke, or operation.
  3. Contact Nanaimo HVAC to schedule a free inspection at no cost to you.
  4. Do not attempt to service or modify the unit yourself.

Need help right now?

Our technicians are trained on this recall and can check your unit's model number and eligibility, and coordinate any needed repair or replacement parts.
